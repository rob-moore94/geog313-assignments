# Assignment 5 Reflection

This was an interesting assignment for sure. I cannot believe we processed 366 scenes + all the precipitation data as easily as we did. The precipitation data is still a bit confusing. First, the station was chosen based on the completeness of data after removing observations with quality flags. This seems like a reasonable approach. All stations within 30 km were assessed. I definitely observe a positive relationship between water-year precipitation and maximum monthly water area (km²), as calculated with NDWI, which is to be expected because more rain means a greater lake water volume.

I don't love how the instructions have us plot monthly median water area and then scatter maximum water area, as it makes it harder to interpret relationships between results. Notably, 2021 has the lowest water area and precipitation. Every year has a dry month (line plot), but 2021 had less rain during typically rainy months. It's amazing how we can track the ecosystem this way.

However, a single station is limited in its true representation of the ecosystem, though, as 1) it's up to 30 km away, and 2) this is only rain measured at a single point source that potentially feeds into the watershed. Now that I am thinking about it, if the station we selected had elevation sloping away from the lake, then there is a chance we may have selected a station representative of a completely different watershed! We should add in elevation data, assess slope angle and direction, and then pick a collection of stations to aggregate together if they are within 30 km and also drain into the lake.

This makes sense because a bunch of rasters is much more data that climatological point data even if its recorded at a high temporal frequency.
